FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is EN590 diesel?

EN 590 is the European standard for automotive diesel fuel. EN590 10 ppm refers to ultra-low sulphur diesel containing no more than 10 mg/kg of sulphur.

What is the difference between diesel and gasoil?

The terms often overlap. Gasoil usually refers to middle distillate grades with higher sulphur limits (for example 50 or 500 ppm) used in industry, power generation or markets with less strict standards.

Do you publish diesel prices?

No. Al Atlas does not publish diesel prices. Offers depend on specification, volume, destination and market conditions and are confirmed in writing for each enquiry.

How is diesel delivered?

Large volumes travel by product tanker under FOB, CFR or CIF terms. Regional deliveries are made by road tanker.

Grades

Bitumen grades on enquiry

Bitumen is the heavy residue of crude oil refining, used as the binder in asphalt. Grades are defined by penetration (hardness at 25 °C) or by viscosity.

Bitumen 60/70Penetration grade 60–70 (0.1 mm at 25 °C). The most widely used paving grade in hot climates.
Bitumen 40/50Harder penetration grade for heavy traffic and high temperatures.
Bitumen 80/100 · 85/100Softer grades for cooler climates and specific mixes.
VG-10 · VG-30 · VG-40Viscosity grades used where specifications follow viscosity classification.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is bitumen 60/70?

Bitumen 60/70 is a penetration grade bitumen whose needle penetration at 25 °C is between 60 and 70 tenths of a millimetre. It is one of the most common paving grades for road construction in warm climates.

Which bitumen packaging is available?

Bitumen can be shipped in steel drums, in bitumen bags or in bulk by heated vessel or road tanker. The right option depends on volume, destination and handling equipment.

Do you publish bitumen prices?

No. Bitumen prices depend on grade, packaging, volume, destination and market conditions, and are confirmed in writing for each enquiry.

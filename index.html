01The company

A UAE trading house for essential commodities.

From the Emirates, at the crossroads of the Gulf, Asia, Africa and Europe, Al Atlas Chemicals Trading connects buyers with the materials that keep energy, farming and manufacturing running.

The company is licensed in the United Arab Emirates for the wholesale trading of refined petroleum products, chemical fertilizers and basic industrial chemicals, together with import and export. Every enquiry is reviewed on its specification, quantity, destination and documentation before an offer is made.

—Logistics

Moved by sea and by road.

Depending on the product, the volume and the destination, deliveries are agreed by sea or by road, under the Incoterm set in the contract.

01
Sea · Liquid bulk

Product tankers

Diesel and other refined fuels travel in bulk on product tankers, loaded and discharged at terminal.

02
Sea · Liquefied gases

Gas carriers

Liquefied gases are shipped on dedicated gas carriers built to keep cargo under pressure or at low temperature.

03
Road · Regional

Road tankers

For regional and inland deliveries, tank trucks complete the last leg to depots, plants and sites.

04How we work

A clear path from enquiry to delivery.

Commodity trade depends on precise specifications and complete paperwork. Each enquiry follows the same disciplined steps.

Prices, availability and delivery terms are never published: they depend on the product, the market and the destination, and are confirmed in writing for each enquiry.
01
Your requirement

You send the product, specification, quantity, destination and preferred delivery terms.

02
Specification and documents review

We check the technical specification, the documents required for the destination and the regulatory points that apply.

03
Written offer

If the request can be served, you receive a written offer stating product, quantity, price basis and delivery terms.

04
Contract and execution

Terms are set by contract. Shipment and documentation follow the agreed Incoterm and payment conditions.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is SN500 base oil?

SN500 is a Group I solvent-neutral base oil with a viscosity of roughly 500 SUS at 100 °F. It is commonly used to blend engine oils and industrial lubricants.

What is the difference between Group I, II and III base oils?

The API groups reflect refining severity. Group I is solvent-refined; Group II is hydrotreated with lower sulphur; Group III is severely hydrocracked with a high viscosity index.

Which packaging is available for base oil?

Base oils can be shipped in drums, flexitanks, ISO tanks or in bulk, depending on volume and destination.
